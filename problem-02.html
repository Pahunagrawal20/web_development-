City Announces New Metro Line Connecting Greater Noida to Delhi

Construction to begin next month, promising faster daily commutes for thousands

Authorities have officially approved the construction of a new metro line connecting Greater Noida directly to central Delhi, aiming to ease the daily commute for thousands of students and working professionals. The project is expected to cut current travel time between the two cities by nearly 40 percent once completed. Officials say this will be one of the most significant infrastructure upgrades for the region in the past decade.

Construction is set to begin at the Greater Noida terminal point, close to several major educational institutions and residential sectors. The new line will pass through key commercial hubs before connecting to the existing Delhi Metro network, allowing seamless interchange for passengers. Officials confirmed that land acquisition for the project has already been completed in most sections.

Work on the project is scheduled to begin on 15 September 2026, with the first phase expected to be operational within three years. The announcement was made at a press briefing held near the proposed site in Greater Noida, where officials also unveiled the initial route map for public review.
