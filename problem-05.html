Science Notes

Chemistry

Water: H2O

Carbon Dioxide: CO2

Oxygen gas: O2

Sulphuric Acid: H2SO4

Glucose: C6H12O6

An atom is the smallest unit of matter that retains the properties of an element.

Water is made up of hydrogen and oxygen atoms bonded together.

Physics

Area is measured in square meters, written as m2

Volume is measured in cubic meters, written as m3

Speed of light = 3 x 108 m/s

Force is defined as mass multiplied by acceleration (F = ma).

The speed of light is considered the fastest speed possible in the universe.

Biology

The human body contains 206 bones in adulthood.

A cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.

DNA carries genetic information in the form of a double helix structure.
