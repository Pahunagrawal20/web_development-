Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

Students must carry their ID card at all times inside the campus.

These rules apply to all students, regardless of semester or branch.

Attendance

A minimum of 75% attendance is mandatory in every subject to be eligible for exams.

Students falling below the required attendance may face a penalty or debarment from exams.

Examination

Carrying mobile phones into the examination hall is strictly prohibited.

Any student found using unfair means during the exam will be marked absent for that paper.

Assignment Submission

All assignments must be submitted through the college portal.
The submission link will only remain active until 11:59 PM on the due date.

Late submissions may still be accepted with a valid reason, at the instructor's discretion.

Discipline

Ragging of any kind is strictly banned and will lead to immediate disciplinary action.

Students are expected to maintain respectful behavior towards faculty and fellow students at all times.
