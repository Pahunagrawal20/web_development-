Slow coffee, honest food, and a corner to breathe.

About Us

The Brewed Leaf started as a small neighborhood coffee cart before growing into a full cafe loved by students and professionals alike. We source our beans directly from small farms and roast in small batches to keep every cup consistent and fresh. Our space is designed to be a quiet retreat from the noise outside — warm lighting, soft music, and just enough buzz of conversation.

Signature Items

Our Caramel Cloud Latte is the most-loved item on the menu, made with double-shot espresso, house-made caramel syrup, and a light, airy foam that holds its shape till the last sip. It pairs perfectly with our freshly baked cinnamon rolls, another café favorite.

The Brewed Café offers delicious signature items like Avocado Toast, Pancakes, Breakfast Sandwiches, Pasta, Grilled Sandwiches and Fresh Salads. These dishes are prepared with fresh ingredients and are perfect for breakfast and lunch.
