City Announces New Metro Line to Ease Traffic Congestion

Construction to Begin Next Month Across Three Major Routes

The city transport authority has officially approved a new metro line aimed at reducing daily traffic congestion in the busiest parts of the city. The project is expected to cost over 300 crore rupees and will be completed in three phases.

Officials confirmed that the construction will begin on 15th September 2026 near the Central Bus Stand area, which is one of the most congested zones in the city.

This move is being seen as a major step toward improving public transport and reducing pollution levels in the region. Local residents have expressed mixed reactions, with many welcoming the decision while some are worried about temporary traffic disruptions during construction.

Local School Wins National Science Competition

Students Recognized for Innovative Water Purification Project

A team of students from a local government school has won first place at the national level science competition held in Delhi. Their project focused on a low-cost water purification system using natural materials.

The competition, held on 20th August 2026, saw participation from over 200 schools across the country.

This achievement highlights the growing interest in practical, science-based problem solving among young students. The winning team will now represent the state at the upcoming international youth science fair.
