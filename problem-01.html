GLA University

About

GLA university established in 1998.The main campus is located in Mathura and second campus located in Greater Noida.This university is accredited with an A+ grade by NAAC and has UGC 12B status.

Programs

GLA University Greater Noida offers various industry-oriented programs, including B.tech in computer science , Artificial Intelligence and machine learning , Electronics, MBA,MCA These programs are designed to provide students with modern technical knowledge, practical skills, and industry exposure.

Campus Life

Campus life at University is vibrant and engaging. Students can participate in various cultural activities such as music, dance, ACT , and student-led events. Students can enjoy sports and athletic activities also. The campus provides opportunities for Students to develop their skills and overall personality beyond academics.

Admission

Admission to GLA university, follows a simple process. Students first need to register online and pay the registration fees. Eligible candidates then appear for the GLA Entrance Test(GLAET), which is an online multiple-choice test. After the result,shortlisted candidates may be required to attend a personal interview, Once slected students can secure their Admission by paying the required token amount and uploading the necessary academic and Identification documents. Admission are open for the 2026 batch.Apply soon to secure your seat.
