Appointment Steps

  1. Select the department and preferred doctor.
  2. Choose a convenient date and time slot.
  3. Fill in patient details and reason for consultation.
  4. Confirm and submit the appointment request.
  5. Receive confirmation via SMS/email from the clinic desk.

Important Terms

Consultation
A scheduled meeting with a doctor to discuss symptoms, diagnosis or treatment.
Follow-up
A subsequent visit to review progress after an initial consultation or treatment.
OPD
Out-Patient Department, where patients are examined without being admitted to the hospital.
Appointment
A confirmed time slot reserved for a patient to meet a specific doctor or avail a service.
