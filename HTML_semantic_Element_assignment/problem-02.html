Engineering & Hiring Process

Our hiring process is designed to be transparent and fair, allowing candidates to showcase both their technical skills and cultural fit.

Note: All shortlisted candidates will receive updates via email within 5 business days of each round.

Hiring Stages

  1. Application submission and resume screening
  2. Initial recruiter screening call
  3. Technical assessment or coding round
  4. Technical interview with the hiring team
  5. HR and culture-fit interview
  6. Final offer discussion and negotiation

Hiring Terms

Screening
An initial review of a candidate's resume and application to check basic eligibility.
Technical Round
An in-depth interview or assessment evaluating a candidate's technical and problem-solving skills.
HR Round
A discussion focused on cultural fit, expectations, compensation and logistics.
Offer
A formal proposal extended to a selected candidate outlining role, compensation and joining details.
