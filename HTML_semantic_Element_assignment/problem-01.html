Admissions

Admission to all programs is based on merit and, for certain programs, an entrance test followed by counselling. Candidates must satisfy the minimum eligibility criteria before applying.

The last date to submit the application is 30th September 2026. Applications received after this date will not be entertained under any circumstances.

Application Process

  1. Register on the admissions portal using a valid email ID.
  2. Fill in personal, academic and program preference details.
  3. Upload scanned copies of required documents.
  4. Pay the application fee online.
  5. Download the submitted application form for your records.
  6. Appear for counselling/interview if shortlisted.

Important Terms

Eligibility
The minimum academic qualification required to apply for a program.
Merit List
A ranked list of candidates prepared on the basis of academic scores or entrance test results.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students based on merit or need.
Application Fee
A non-refundable fee paid at the time of submitting the application form.
