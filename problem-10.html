About Institution

Techstar University was established in 1965 and has since grown into one of the most respected institutions in the region for higher education. Spread across 200 acres, the university offers a blend of academic rigor and practical exposure across Engineering, Management, Sciences, and Arts. Our mission is to nurture critical thinkers who are ready to contribute meaningfully to society and industry alike.

Principal/Director Message

"Education is not just about degrees; it's about shaping character and capability." - Dr. Ramesh Kapoor, Director

Under Dr. Kapoor's leadership, the university has expanded its research output and industry collaborations significantly over the past decade. He continues to emphasize holistic development, encouraging students to engage in academics, sports, and community service equally.

Academic Programs

Techstar University offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs designed to meet current industry demands.

Engineering Programs

Our Engineering programs cover Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Mechanical, and Civil Engineering, with strong emphasis on lab work and industry projects.

Science Programs

Science students explore core concepts through hands-on lab work; for instance, Chemistry students regularly study molecular structures such as H2O and CO2 to understand chemical bonding in practice.

Campus Facilities

The campus houses a 24/7 central library, multiple computer labs, sports complexes, and well-equipped hostels for both boys and girls. Our indoor auditorium hosts seminars and cultural programs throughout the year, while outdoor sports facilities include a football ground, basketball courts, and a swimming pool.

All facilities are maintained to ensure a safe and productive environment for students and staff alike.

Student Life

Student life at Techstar is vibrant, with over 40 clubs and societies ranging from robotics and coding to music, drama, and debate. The annual cultural fest, Zenith, and the technical fest, Innovate, are the biggest highlights of the academic calendar, drawing participation from colleges across the region.

Students are strongly encouraged to participate in at least one extracurricular activity during their time here, as it contributes to overall personality development.

Admission Information

Admission to Techstar University is based on entrance examination scores, academic performance, and a personal interview for select programs. All admission forms must be submitted along with self-attested copies of academic certificates; incomplete applications will not be considered.

The last date to submit online applications for the upcoming academic year is September 30, 2026. Applicants are advised to apply well before the deadline to avoid last-minute technical issues.
