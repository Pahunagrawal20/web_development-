What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a technology that allows computers to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

AI is used in many areas such as education, healthcare, smartphones and online services. It can help computers understand information and solve problems.

AI is becoming an important part of modern technology.

Cloud Computing

Cloud computing allows users to store data and use computer services over the internet.

Instead of keeping everything on a personal computer, users can use cloud services for storage, applications and computing resources. Cloud computing is widely used by businesses and students.

Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks and information from unauthorized access and other online threats.

Strong passwords, software updates and careful use of links can help people protect their accounts and personal information.
