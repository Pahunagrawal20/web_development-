Hiring Process

Our recruitment process normally follows these stages:

  1. Submit your job application.
  2. Application screening by the recruitment team.
  3. Technical assessment or interview.
  4. Technical interview round.
  5. Human Resources interview.
  6. Final selection and offer.

Recruitment Stage Definitions

Screening
The recruitment team reviews the candidate's application, resume and qualifications.
Technical Round
Candidates are evaluated on their technical knowledge, problem-solving ability and job-related skills.
HR Round
The HR team discusses communication skills, career goals, company culture and other employment details.
Offer
Selected candidates receive an employment offer containing important job and compensation details.
