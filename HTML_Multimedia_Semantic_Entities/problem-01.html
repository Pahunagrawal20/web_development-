Admission Process

Follow these steps to complete your admission application:

  1. Visit the university admission portal.
  2. Create a new student account.
  3. Enter your personal details.
  4. Enter your academic details.
  5. Select your preferred program.
  6. Upload the required documents.
  7. Pay the application fee.
  8. Submit the application form.
  9. Check the merit list and admission status.

Important Admission Information

Eligibility
Eligibility depends on the selected program and the applicant's previous educational qualification.
Scholarship
Scholarships may be provided to eligible students based on academic merit and university policies.
Merit List
The merit list contains candidates selected according to academic performance and admission criteria.
Application Fee
Applicants must pay the applicable application fee during the online application process.
