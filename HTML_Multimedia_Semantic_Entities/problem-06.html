Our Restaurant Story

Spice Garden started with a simple idea: to serve fresh, tasty and affordable food in a welcoming environment. Our chefs prepare every dish using quality ingredients and traditional cooking methods.

We provide dine-in, takeaway and home delivery services for our customers.

Chef Profile

Chef Aarav Mehta

Chef Aarav Mehta is an experienced chef who specializes in Indian cuisine. He combines traditional recipes with modern presentation to create memorable meals.

His cooking philosophy is: fresh ingredients, balanced flavours and careful preparation.
