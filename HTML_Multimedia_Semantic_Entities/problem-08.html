Introduction to Personal Finance

Personal finance means managing your money wisely. It includes saving, spending, investing and borrowing money responsibly.

"Do not save what is left after spending, but spend what is left after saving."

Learning basic financial concepts can help you make better decisions about your money.

Bank Accounts

Savings Account

A savings account is designed for saving money and usually provides interest on the deposited amount.

Current Account

A current account is mainly useful for businesses and people who make frequent transactions.

Fixed Deposit

A fixed deposit allows you to keep money with a bank for a fixed period and earn interest.

Understanding Cards

Debit Card

A debit card allows you to spend money directly from your bank account.

Credit Card

A credit card allows you to borrow money up to a specified credit limit and repay it later.

Card Safety Notice

Never share your PIN, CVV, OTP or banking password with anyone.

Understanding Loans

What is a Loan?

A loan is money borrowed from a bank or financial institution that is repaid over an agreed period, usually with interest.

Common Types of Loans

  • Education Loan
  • Home Loan
  • Personal Loan
  • Vehicle Loan
  • Business Loan

Important Disclaimer

Loan rates and charges vary between banks and customers. Always read the official terms and conditions before taking a financial product.

Responsible Financial Tips

  • Create a monthly budget.
  • Save a portion of your income regularly.
  • Avoid unnecessary debt.
  • Pay bills and EMIs on time.
  • Keep your banking information private.
  • Build an emergency fund.
  • Compare interest rates before borrowing.
  • Read financial documents carefully.

Steps to Open a Bank Account

  1. Choose a suitable bank.
  2. Select the type of account.
  3. Fill in the account-opening form.
  4. Submit required KYC documents.
  5. Complete the verification process.
  6. Receive your account details and banking facilities.

Important Financial Terms

Interest Rate
The percentage charged or earned on money.
EMI
Equated Monthly Instalment paid towards a loan.
Principal
The original amount of money borrowed or invested.
Tenure
The period for which a loan or investment is held.
